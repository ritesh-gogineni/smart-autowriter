# Niche pack: <your niche>

A niche pack tells the scout, writer and checker what is worth writing about in your subject. Copy this file to `niches/<name>/niche.md`, fill in each section, and set `"niche": "<name>"` in `tiered-writer.config.json`. Delete the guidance in italics once you have filled a section in.

## What the data says
*Your own evidence, not guesses: which formats, topics and times earn clicks on your site. Put real numbers here and say when they were measured. The scout treats this section as the tie-breaker when its own judgement disagrees. If you have no data yet, say so and list your hypotheses as hypotheses.*

## Formats and word ranges
*A table or list: format name, typical query shape, word range, graphic that fits. Example: 'Product comparison | "X vs Y" | 700-1000 | comparison table'.*

## Sources
*Official or primary sources the scout and checker should trust first, and sources they must never use (aggregators, tips sites, fan blogs).*

## Volatile vs stable facts
*Which kinds of fact change quickly (these are re-verified after 90 minutes) and which are stable (re-verified after 24 hours). Also list any live data feed whose numbers must never be cached.*

## Calendar and key dates
*Events, deadlines or release dates that drive search demand in the next weeks. Refresh weekly.*

## Timing
*When your readers are online, and what to publish when few are. Time zone of the audience.*

## Experiments
*Topic types you have not tried much. The pipeline uses these when the run mode is EXPERIMENT. Optionally, a 'DISCOVER ATTEMPT' definition if you chase Google Discover.*

## Extra style rules
*Niche-specific banned words or phrases, spelling conventions, how to name things.*

## Categories and tags
*Which categories on your site map to which subjects.*
